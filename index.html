About Me

The person behind the programs

GH

I'm Gwenevere Hubbard, Mia to most people. I lead programs at the intersection of marketing technology, data, and AI: CDP implementations, platform rollouts, release governance, and the messy cross-functional work that turns roadmaps into shipped outcomes.

My career spans top-tier consulting at Accenture (serving Coca-Cola, Meta, and Cigna), enterprise restaurant brands at Darden and Checkers & Rally's, and global program delivery for GM's OnStar. I lead through influence in highly matrixed organizations, aligning 20+ stakeholders across countries, time zones, and competing priorities.

Right now I'm pairing that delivery record with an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Georgetown, embedding LLM tooling into program workflows, most recently cutting reporting cycle time 40% with generative AI.
