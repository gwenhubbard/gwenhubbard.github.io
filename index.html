The Approach

Growth lives in the seams between four disciplines.

Most leaders own one of these. The value I create comes from working all four at once, because a weakness in any one of them quietly caps the other three.

Platforms without operations stall. A CDP nobody can run is an invoice, not an asset.

Operations without data guess. Process discipline only pays when the measurement underneath it is honest.

Transformation without adoption fails. The launch is the easy part. Getting a franchise system or three countries to actually use it is the work.

AI without any of the above is theater. Generative AI compounds a working system and exposes a broken one.

So I build the stack, run the machine, lead the change, and use AI to compound all three.

About

I am Gwenevere Hubbard, Mia to most people. I spend my time in the space between marketing, engineering, and data, where the interesting problems live.

My career runs from enterprise brands to top-tier consulting: CRM and MarTech leadership at Darden and Checkers & Rally's, global program delivery for GM's OnStar, and technology program delivery at Accenture serving Coca-Cola, Meta, and Cigna. Along the way I have owned a $10M+ technology portfolio, launched a CDP from zero to one, and rebuilt the operating model of delivery teams that had stopped shipping.

The through line is convergence. I have never been only the platform person or only the process person. I get pulled in when the stack, the operations, and the people have drifted apart and someone has to pull them back together into something that produces revenue.

Now I am adding the fourth discipline deliberately. I am completing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Georgetown, I embed generative AI in the delivery workflows I lead, and I build and teach AI agents: writing the playbooks that show teams how to use generative AI and how to build agents of their own.

Building AI agents that run the work unattended

Agents

Read the story
Situation

The recurring multi-step workflows around research, document tailoring, and tracking consumed hours and produced inconsistent results, because every run depended on whoever was doing it that day.

Task

Automate the entire workflow rather than accelerating individual steps with one-off prompts.

Action

Designed and built AI agents that chain the full process end to end: retrieving source material, applying scoring and formatting rules, producing finished deliverables, logging results to avoid duplicate work, and running unattended on a schedule.

Result
